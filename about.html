About · Our Story

Nearly 60 years of Jewish education in Morris County.

Founded in 1967 as Hebrew Academy of Morris County, GRTWA is the only Jewish community day school in Northwestern New Jersey.

Our mission

Lifelong learners, compassionate individuals, rooted in their heritage.

Gottesman RTW Academy maintains high standards of academic excellence while nurturing each student's social and emotional needs and growth. As a community day school, GRTWA celebrates the richness of Jewish life, has a strong connection to Israel, and welcomes families from a wide range of Jewish backgrounds.

Accredited by and a member of the New Jersey Association of Independent Schools (NJAIS); member of the National Association of Independent Schools (NAIS) and Prizmah. Awarded a Blue Ribbon by the U.S. Department of Education.

Timeline
1966

Two Morristown couples, Morty z"l and Beatsy z"l Wertheimer and Charlotte and Alvin z"l Turner, share a vision for a Jewish day school, joined by Michael z"l and Midge z"l Rubenstein.

1967

Hebrew Academy of Morris County (HAMC) is founded.

Green campus

A state-of-the-art green facility on a 22-acre Randolph campus, with solar panels, rain gardens, and flexible, technologically advanced classrooms.

Today

Renamed Gottesman RTW Academy, honoring the founding families (the Rubensteins, Turners, and Wertheimers) and Paula and Jerry Gottesman, whose $15 million challenge gift provided transformative support.

What's in a name

R · T · W

The "RTW" honors the three founding families, the Rubensteins, the Turners, and the Wertheimers, whose vision, dedication, and belief in the power of Jewish education laid the foundation for the vibrant community that exists today.

Head of School

A welcome from Rav Will Keller

At Gottesman RTW Academy, we believe that children thrive when school and family work together. That partnership is at the heart of everything we do, helping each child grow into a curious learner, compassionate leader, proud Jew, and caring member of the community.

Learning at GRTWA is both joyful and challenging. Our exceptional faculty inspire curiosity, encourage critical thinking, and nurture each child's unique strengths.

Whether you are exploring Jewish day school for the first time or are already part of our family, I invite you to visit our campus to meet our students and faculty. I look forward to welcoming you personally.

B'Shalom, Rav Will Keller

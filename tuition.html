Tuition & Affordability

Every Jewish child deserves the gift of a day school education.

Through TuitionMAX, families on assistance will never pay more than 14% of their adjusted gross income toward K–8 tuition, no matter how many children are enrolled.

Illustration only, not an offer of aid. Per the school's published policy, TuitionMAX caps a family's K–8 obligation at no more than 14% of AGI, including ECC siblings of K–8 students. Assistance is based on need and available funds, and families must enroll and provide financial information to the Tuition Assistance Committee. ECC-only families can apply for partial scholarships through several small funds. Fees are not included.

How it works

Affordability is central to who we are.

  • Applications are strictly confidential and handled with dignity and compassion
  • Assistance is available for families with children in grades K–8
  • GRTWA subscribes to the NAIS principles of fair practice for financial aid
  • Tuition covers approximately 70% of the cost of educating each child; giving closes the gap

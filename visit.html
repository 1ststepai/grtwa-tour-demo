The admissions path

GRTWA works on a rolling admissions basis. You are welcome to apply at any time for the current or following school year.

1 · Inquiry

Request information

The admissions team will call to talk about your family's needs and schedule a visit.

2 · Apply

Online application

$75 non-refundable fee per child, plus records, health forms, and birth certificate.

3 · Child visit

Meet the team

For children entering three-year-olds through 8th grade.

4 · Decision

Offer & registration

Notification by phone and email; 3 weeks to accept the position and any assistance offer.

Kindergarten eligibility: a child must turn 5 by October 31 of the entry year. At least one parent must be Jewish.
